# Original icon — Prism Launcher

Four original vector layers: a violet category card, an aqua launch card, a dark crystal front pane, and a white app/launch-direction mark. The fanned silhouette expresses organized applications and movement into a chosen app. No third-party mark or raster artwork used.

Icon Composer supplies native Liquid Glass, specular highlights, translucency and shadows. The bright white mark sits on a dark blue pane so it stays legible against both default-indigo and dark backgrounds. Native Default, Dark and Mono previews were inspected in Icon Composer with generation27 enabled. The same layered structure produces clear/tinted appearances automatically.

AppIcon-Default.png is the native1024×1024 flattened export; AppIcon.png is the small ICNS-derived preview. AppIcon.icon remains the editable layered source and compiles with actool. Source groups render front to back, unlike a back-to-front drawing API; initial order was corrected after inspecting the compiled result. Developer identity logos in About remain unchanged.

Sources: https://developer.apple.com/icon-composer/ and https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons.
